## Start playing

Open a movie, episode, track, audiobook, recording or live channel, and choose its main play action. Portico asks the server for a playback decision that suits your device and the settings you've selected.

## Resume on another device

Your progress is stored under the active profile on that server. Stop on one device, then use the same server and profile in another connected Portico app to resume the item.

Progress is saved as playback is confirmed by the player. Stop or close playback while connected so the server can receive the latest position. Pausing watch history also pauses progress updates. Very short sessions, and items near the beginning or end, can be handled differently so that accidental starts don't fill up **Continue Watching**.

## Player controls

The controls you see depend on the media and the device. They can include:

- play and pause
- previous and next
- short skip controls
- volume
- audio tracks
- subtitles or lyrics
- playback quality
- queue
- fullscreen
- close
